INTERPRETATION / LIMITS
This is a geometric observation map, not yet a stiffness/coupling matrix.
The result depends on the explicitly stated sensor convention and edge frame orientation.
The 36-row map stores only the three edge-integrated strains; the 180-row profile map stores all coefficients (powers 0..4) of the three strain profiles on each edge.
K_edge is the exact L2 Gram stiffness with unit axial/shear weights; any strictly positive axial/shear weights preserve its kernel and rank.
No dynamical/Krylov/PBH conclusion follows from this edge rank alone.
The distributed edge stiffness is K_edge=L^T W L; it retains profile information and must not be replaced by A_integrated^T W A_integrated if spatial variation matters.
